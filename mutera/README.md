# Mutera Engine

Mutera is an evolutionary sandbox built around organisms, genomes, environments and populations.

## Current foundation

- Genome and mutation
- Organisms, energy, health and age
- Reproduction
- Environments and resources
- Populations and world simulation
- Statistics
- Automated tests

## Planned systems

1. Genetic traits and inheritance
2. Natural selection and fitness
3. Ecosystems and species
4. Predators, competition and symbiosis
5. Events and discoveries
6. Persistent world state
7. Progression and achievements
8. Telegram interface
